Welcome to HomeStew

A few one-time steps — you can skip any of them.

Encrypt stored credentials

HomeStew couldn't find a secrets master key. Without one, credentials you save (API keys, webhook URLs and tokens) are stored as plaintext in the data volume.

Creating it generates a random key inside your data volume (/data/.secrets_key). This is a one-time step — after it, every saved secret is encrypted at rest. You can also manage the key later under Settings > Advanced.

Connect your AI model

Chat and manual search run on an OpenAI-compatible server you control (Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, or a hosted provider). Point HomeStew at it and pick a model. You can change this later under Settings > AI.

For Ollama on the same machine as the container, use your host's address (e.g. http://host.docker.internal:11434/v1).

Click Models to load the list from your server.

Add your first device

HomeStew organises manuals, search and maintenance reminders around your devices. Add one now — manuals can be downloaded or uploaded later from the device's Edit dialog.
